About Chi Mei

A connected healthcare system serving southern Taiwan

Chi Mei is a nonprofit healthcare system based in Tainan, with approximately 2,500 beds across three hospitals. Our network brings together tertiary, regional, and community hospital care, alongside services that support patients at home and in long-term care.

Care beyond the hospital

The foundation’s services also include one postpartum care center, three home nursing agencies, and three integrated long-term care facilities.

04 / The strategy in practice

Find the right patients. Prioritize the right change. Coordinate the right care.

Our approach combines a shared platform, a standard care pathway, and a trained multidisciplinary team. The aim is to turn fragmented specialty visits into coordinated care that learns from each patient encounter.

Identify risk systematically

The platform brings together records, tests, claims, and patient questionnaires to show each patient’s risk over time. Updated risk indicators appear in the medical record as traffic-light prompts for enrollment and referral.

Match support to need

Lower-risk patients receive prevention and lifestyle support. Patients needing closer follow-up enter case-manager-led care. Complex or high-risk cases receive multidisciplinary review and coordinated intervention.

Make behavior change followable

Every follow-up visit connects assessment, personalized advice, an agreed goal, and review. The next visit closes the loop and creates new data for the next decision.

The care pathway is embedded in daily work

Screen and classify risk → check in → assess → case-manager counseling → physician consultation → reinforce the plan → complete claims → remind and follow up.

This standard pathway connects clinical care, documentation, and administration. Case managers coordinate the steps and referrals.

05 / How we currently work

A learning opportunity in every follow-up visit

1 · Assess

Collect data

A 3–5 minute assessment covers diet, activity, sleep, stress and emotions, tobacco and alcohol, and medication adherence.

2 · Interpret

Identify priorities

AI compares clinical values and trends. The case manager reviews suggestions and discusses the most relevant change with the patient.

3 · Act

Agree on a goal

Set a specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound (SMART) goal and tailor support to the patient’s risk and circumstances.

4 · Review

Learn from results

At the next visit, review progress and barriers. Record the result and adapt the plan.

The next operational step: make this patient-level loop produce reliable team-level learning—what changed, for whom, under which conditions, and what should the care team do differently next month?
